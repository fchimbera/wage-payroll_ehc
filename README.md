# wage-payroll_ehc
Wage based payroll system prototype designed Eastern height college.

## Technologies
PHP, JAVASCRIPT, HTML5, CSS, MYSQL

## Payroll Management System
### Business Need:
-	To provide an efficient and effective way to manage payroll transactions and data.
### Business Requirements:
-	To automate the calculation of employee work hours.
-	Secure all payroll data.
-	Provide financial reports and forecasting.
-	Provide quick access to business Tax and NSSA information.
-	Minimise student interaction with the system but still provide accurate data.
### Business Value:
Benefits
-	Reduces time delays during lesson change-over.
-	Increases data accuracy.
-	Reduces forging of work hours.
-	Provides monthly and annual reports.
-	Provides a basis for financial forecasting.
-	To ensure the business remains complaint with IRS tax laws and changes.
-	Better suited to meet deadlines and pay dates.
Constraints
-	It is impossible to account for work done outside of work area.
-	Sole dependent on the availability of an internet connection.
-	Follows rigid guidelines and rules set in the system which make it difficult to accommodate for administrational delays i.e. meeting, conferences.
-	Raises security concerns in terms of leaks and hacking.
-	Raises accountability concerns where computing errors are found either in wage data or tax and NSSA data.
